# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Treeframe to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or nationality. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space agrees to help keep it respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
* Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the code or the idea, never the person.
* Assume good intentions and ask questions before jumping to conclusions.
* Respect different opinions, approaches and languages. Contributions in English or Portuguese are welcome.
* Credit other people's work and help.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
* Discriminatory jokes, slurs or exclusionary language.
* Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
* Spam, trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, and also to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may take fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate. This can include editing or removing comments, closing or locking issues and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

To report a problem, email [gluedson.dev@gmail.com](mailto:gluedson.dev@gmail.com). Reports are handled privately, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

Maintainers who do not follow this Code of Conduct may face consequences decided by the rest of the project leadership.
